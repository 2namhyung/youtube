# Audiovisual rhythm

## Principles

- Narration carries the claim; imagery carries evidence.
- A click, stamp, page turn, or tonal hit may mark one visible state change.
- Synchronized sound can attract attention, so reserve it for editorially important changes. Source: [audiovisual-attention study](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#audiovisual-synchrony)
- Beat-synchronized repetition is suitable for counts or accumulation only when the visual quantity is truthful. The rhythmic-accumulation precedent is observable in the White Stripes reference. Source: [visual references](../../raw/sources/visual-reference-works-2026-08-21.md#match-repetition-parallel-frames-screen-narrative)
- Background music is removable. If comprehension declines without it, the visual structure is incomplete.

## Avoid

- Sound on every movement
- Trailer impacts under routine facts
- Music that falsely escalates uncertainty into guilt or danger
- On-screen prose that duplicates the entire narration
