# Evidence comparison

## Purpose

Show differences, concurrency, or provenance without implying unsupported causality.

## Rules

1. Match cuts require at least two shared properties among shape, position, direction, and meaning. This is a 23jung editorial safeguard inferred from the graphic-match reference. Source: [match-cut references](../../raw/sources/visual-reference-works-2026-08-21.md#match-repetition-parallel-frames-screen-narrative)
2. Split screens are reserved for comparisons that must be visible simultaneously.
3. Only one panel is active; supporting panels are visually quieter.
4. Labels distinguish official source, press report, testimony, and editorial inference.
5. Shared motion must not convert correlation into causality. Animation can create false identity or causality when semantics do not support it. Source: [transition research](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#animated-data-transitions)

## Recommended layouts

- Before / after text
- Statement / official record
- Complaint trend / policy timeline
- Confirmed / unresolved
