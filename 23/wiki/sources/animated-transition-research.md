## Claims and evidence

- Well-designed animated transitions can improve perception between related statistical graphics. Source: [Heer & Robertson capture](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#animated-data-transitions)
- Preserving object identity is useful, but transforming unrelated objects can manufacture a false relationship. Source: [Heer & Robertson capture](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#animated-data-transitions)
- Staging complex changes and keeping one meaningful action at a time supports tracking. Source: [Heer & Robertson capture](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#animated-data-transitions)
- Bundled paths may aid tracking when several nearby objects move similarly, especially with occlusion or deformation. Source: [IBM research capture](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#animated-data-transitions)
- A synchronized visible and audible event can act as an attention cue. Source: [audiovisual-attention study](../../raw/sources/animated-transitions-and-sound-2026-08-21.md#audiovisual-synchrony)
