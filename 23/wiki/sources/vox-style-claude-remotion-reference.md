The tutorial demonstrates a narration-led editorial collage assembled in Remotion: textured backgrounds, photo cut-outs, documents, charts, large numeric objects, and short purposeful transitions. ## Observed production grammar

1. **Narration line first.** The scene breakdown is organized by voiceover line, then background, midground asset, foreground asset, prompts, and transition. Evidence: [scene table at 01:30](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-01-30.jpg).
2. **A locked visual world.** Scenes reuse a paper/grid/print texture, so different media still feel like one program. Evidence: [tanker collage at 00:15](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-00-15.jpg), [chart at 00:30](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-00-30.jpg).
3. **Layered editorial assets.** A scene is composed from background, midground, and foreground rather than one finished image. Cut-out people, architecture, ocean footage, publication pages, numbers, and marker accents can therefore move independently. Evidence: [scene table at 01:30](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-01-30.jpg), [Remotion preview at 07:57](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-07-57.jpg).
4. **Data is a visual object.** Charts draw themselves and numbers change in the frame; they are not merely captions beside a decorative picture. Evidence: [CPI chart at 00:30](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-00-30.jpg).
5. **Each scene is tunable.** The tutorial describes scene-specific components with props for position, scale, timing, and color, while retaining the common background. Evidence: [component/prop explanation at 03:38](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-03-38.jpg), [Remotion Studio adjustment at 07:57](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-07-57.jpg).
6. **Voiceover controls the edit.** Scenes begin and end with their narration line, and important cuts land on spoken words. Evidence: [timing instructions at 12:00](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-12-00.jpg).
7. **Transitions explain continuity.** Hard cuts, slides, and object-preserving motion are selected in the scene plan; transition spectacle is not the subject. Evidence: [transition column at 01:30](../../raw/assets/vox-style-reference/00-01-30.jpg).

## 23jung adaptation

For political explainers, the same grammar maps naturally to `daily-life problem → policy change → responsible institution/legislator → primary document → measurable effect`. Official documents, news pages, parliamentary video, maps, charts, and politician cut-outs become the principal screen objects. AI-generated images or clips are reserved for clearly labelled reconstruction when no authentic visual exists; they are supporting assets, not the default scene background.

Every scene must be specified with:

`claim / voiceover / background / midground / foreground / source / transition / sound cue / verification state / AI disclosure`
