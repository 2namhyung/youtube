## Claims and evidence

- Signaling can direct attention toward essential material. Source: [Brame review](../../raw/sources/multimedia-learning-2026-08-21.md#sources)
- Segmenting can help manage complex information, while irrelevant music, backgrounds, or decoration can add extraneous load. Source: [Brame review](../../raw/sources/multimedia-learning-2026-08-21.md#sources)
- Related words and visuals should be close in space and time; narration and on-screen text should not automatically duplicate one another. Source: [CTML implementation article](../../raw/sources/multimedia-learning-2026-08-21.md#sources)
- Evidence strength differs among principles, so this wiki does not treat every multimedia-learning recommendation as universal law. Source: [AI video assistant review](../../raw/sources/multimedia-learning-2026-08-21.md#sources)

## Application inference

Use chapters, active-card highlighting, and complementary narration/visuals. Test the rules with politically uninterested viewers rather than assuming that classroom findings transfer unchanged.
