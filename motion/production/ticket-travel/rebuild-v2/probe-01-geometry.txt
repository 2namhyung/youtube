Input: probe-01-omni.mp4, 720x1280, 24fps, 96 frames, 4.000s.

Using decoded PNG frames and a threshold min(R,G,B)<235, the non-white outer extent was identical at sampled frames f=0,8,...,88:
  x=113..628, y=60..1244
This corresponds to approximately x=.157.. .872 and y=.047.. .972 in normalized probe coordinates. It indicates a strongly locked card rectangle in this clip; it is not a reference-scale claim because the probe is 720x1280 while the reference is 1080x2160.

Visual review of the 5x4 contact and a 2x crop: card typography, barcode, rounded frame and shadow remain fixed; the embedded pink-dress figure changes pose. The figure is cleanly integrated at this scale; no obvious white matte/halo boundary was visible around head, arm, dress or legs in the crop. This is a qualitative observation, not a pixel-alpha test.

Limitations / no false PASS:
- Probe contains one Bangkok card only; it does not test the initial backpack walk or any slide transition/stacked-card alignment.
- A 4s single-card clip cannot establish cross-card identity consistency, inter-card walking continuity, or transition easing.
- Person bbox was not robustly separable from printed ticket text, so no numeric person-scale PASS is asserted. The contact shows pose motion with approximately stable apparent scale, but this needs a masked person layer or landmark measurement in the next probe.
