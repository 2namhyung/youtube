# source-hanbok.mp4 vs edit-kimono.mp4 — visual verification

Evidence: [paired-sheet.png](evidence/paired-sheet.png) (top 8 source frames, bottom 8 edit frames; sampled every 12 frames / 0.5 s), plus [source-sheet.png](evidence/source-sheet.png) and [edit-sheet.png](evidence/edit-sheet.png). Both clips are 720×1280, 24 fps, 96 frames / 4.00 s, so the samples are timestamp-matched.

## Findings

- **Gait / foot-contact synchronization:** The broad walk cycle and pose progression appear aligned at the sampled timestamps: both clips move through the same standing/step/cross-step sequence and finish in comparable upright poses. Exact heel/toe contact cannot be certified from 0.5-second samples; the hem (hanbok) and long kimono panels obscure portions of the feet. A frame-by-frame contact audit would be needed to establish one-frame timing.
- **Head / pelvis screen placement:** Head position and overall body placement stay visually close across the matched frames (near the same horizontal center and top/bottom margins). The apparent waist/pelvis line is not directly comparable because the hanbok skirt begins with a high, soft waist while the kimono has a broad obi; no reliable joint coordinates were measured.
- **Apparent anatomy vs clothing silhouette:** Head/torso scale looks consistent, but the garment silhouette changes substantially: the hanbok flares widely below the waist and swings laterally, whereas the kimono is a straighter, narrower column with wide sleeves and an obi. Any perceived body-width or hip change is therefore clothing-driven; this review does not establish anatomical deformation.
- **Props / background:** The white seamless studio background and floor shadow remain consistent. Visible accessories are not preserved as a strict clothing-only substitution: source shows the colorful wrapped prop in the viewer-right hand plus a hanging waist ornament and embroidered flats; edit shows a colorful pouch in the viewer-left hand, no equivalent hanging waist ornament, and different sandals. Hand/arm pose and sleeve geometry also visibly differ.

## Limitations

This is a visual spot check from eight matched timestamps and does not infer exact joints, pixel offsets, or per-frame foot-contact timing. The generated side-by-side comparison video was not duplicated.
