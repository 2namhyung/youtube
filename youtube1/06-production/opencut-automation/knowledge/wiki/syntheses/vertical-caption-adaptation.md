# Vertical caption adaptation

The reference's useful pattern is not its landscape line length but its stable semantic blocks. For this Shorts pipeline, each source narration cue remains authoritative, then is divided into consecutive one-line child cues of at most 16 graphemes. Child timings divide the source interval by readable grapheme weight, so every spoken word remains represented exactly once. Source: [[../sources/reference-video-K2MWaSrkP4I]] and [[../concepts/caption-generation-rules]].

The existing visual design remains unchanged: centered white text, black outline, no background. Source: [operator decisions](../../raw/inbox/operator-caption-decisions.md#locked-decisions).
