# Storyboard-guided all-video-model design v1

Status: preproduction-review-only  
Project: `research-6b1a8af9b4143acb`  
Duration authority: approved owner narration, 55.016 seconds  
Provider rule: one approved rough storyboard for every cut; human cuts add only the approved recurring character masters listed below. The storyboard controls composition, place, posture and action direction. It never controls identity, final appearance, readable text or exact first-frame pixels.

## Global visual rule

- Every storyboard is one square scene, not a multi-panel sheet.
- Storyboard people are blank faceless mannequins. Uploaded character masters are the only identity and appearance authority.
- Exact Korean text, legal qualifiers, dates, statuses, source disclosures and title/captions are deterministic post overlays.
- No environment, prop, document, map, marker, divider, timeline, grid or effect PNG may be uploaded to the video provider.
- Never invent successful filming, captured imagery, phone-breaking/removal method, hiding, seized items, entry/search procedure, arrest, handcuffs, guilt, sentence or final legal outcome.
- Each provider source must show one forward state change without reversal. Independent semantic QA selects the authored 2-4 second continuous window.
