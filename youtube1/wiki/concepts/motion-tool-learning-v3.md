## Learned grammar

The verified engines are GSAP, Motion Canvas, Canvas/D3, Lottie-web, dotLottie-web and FFmpeg keyframes. Each produced 972x972, 24fps, 48-frame samples for ten different mechanics: translation, scale, quarter-turn rotation, opacity reveal, three-object stagger, progressive draw, horizontal wipe, closed orbit, bounce/settle and translate-then-scale.

Similarity is semantic and observable. Ten independently measured motion properties define each example, and a sample passes at nine or more agreements. Pixel copying, shared artwork, branding and source-code copying do not count as learning.
